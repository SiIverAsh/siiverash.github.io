Research Interests

I work on making large vision-language models (LVLMs) more faithful to what they actually see. My current focus is object hallucination — models describing objects that are not in the image — and how to suppress it without flattening the richness of the generated text.

Topics: object hallucination, hallucination mitigation, visual evidence injection, decoding-time intervention, dynamic gating, logit-level control, multimodal evaluation benchmarks.
